Privacy Policy

Sahas NX mobile application

Last updated: 23 October 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how information may be collected, used, stored and shared when you use the Sahas NX mobile application. It also explains how to contact us with questions or concerns about privacy.

1. About Sahas NX

Sahas NX is an organisational application for authorised users. It provides access to services and information associated with a user's organisational account. Depending on the access granted, this may include employee information and reports such as salary slips, ledger reports, leave details and other service-related records.

2. Information that may be processed

When you sign in to or use Sahas NX, the application and its connected systems may process information needed to provide its services. This information may include:

  • Account information, such as login credentials or an employee identifier used to verify your access.
  • Organisational records made available to your account, which may include employment details, salary or payroll information, leave details, ledger reports or other service records.
  • Technical information needed to operate, protect, maintain or troubleshoot the application, where collected by the application or its supporting systems.
  • Information you provide when using a feature or contacting the support team.

The information processed depends on the features available to your account and the systems connected to the application.

3. How information is used

Information may be used to verify your identity, provide access to authorised features, retrieve and display relevant organisational records, maintain the application's security and reliability, investigate technical issues, respond to support requests, and meet applicable legal or organisational requirements.

4. Storage and sharing

Sahas NX may connect to the organisation's backend systems to authenticate users and retrieve information needed by the application. Information may be processed by the app and by the systems that support it.

Information may be accessed by authorised personnel and approved service providers when necessary to operate or support the application. It may also be disclosed when required by law. The organisation's actual data-sharing practices and service-provider arrangements should be reflected in this policy.

5. Information security

Reasonable technical and organisational safeguards are used to help protect information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse, alteration or disclosure. Users should keep their login details confidential and report suspected unauthorised access through the official support channel. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

6. Data retention and requests

Information is retained in accordance with applicable organisational requirements and record-retention policies. If you have questions about information associated with your account, or wish to request its correction or deletion where applicable, please contact the organisation using the details below. Certain official, employment or financial records may need to be retained to meet legal or administrative requirements.

7. App permissions

Sahas NX may request access to device functions when a feature requires it. You can review the app's permissions in your device settings. Disabling a permission may affect the related feature.

8. Children's privacy

Sahas NX is intended for authorised organisational users and is not directed at children.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the application, its services or applicable requirements. Any revised version will be published on this page, and the date at the top will be updated.

10. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how information is handled through Sahas NX, please contact the authorised application support team.
